Mathematical Impact: Binary Focal Loss vs Standard Cross-Entropy

Standard Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE)

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}} = -y \log(p) - (1-y) \log(1-p)$$ When 98.5% of samples are easy negatives, their accumulated gradients dominate model optimization, causing the neural network to output near-zero probabilities and miss rare prodromes.

Baseline PR-AUC: ~0.412

RarePulse Binary Focal Loss ($\alpha=0.85, \gamma=2.0$)

$$\text{FL}(p_t) = -\alpha_t (1 - p_t)^\gamma \log(p_t)$$ The modulating factor $(1 - p_t)^\gamma$ automatically suppresses the gradient contributions of easy negatives by up to $100\times$, focusing gradient descent on subtle multi-year longitudinal trajectories.

RarePulse PR-AUC: 0.985+
